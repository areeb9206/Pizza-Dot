How we handle your order data

When you place an order, Pizza Dot collects the name, email, phone number, delivery address, selected items, payment reference and any extra instructions you supply. Order information is saved to Firebase Realtime Database.

Payment screenshots are compressed and saved within restricted Firebase Realtime Database records, not publicly accessible image URLs. Only the authenticated account that submitted the order and authorized restaurant administrators are intended to access its screenshot and details.

Firebase Authentication provides email/password account access. FormSubmit receives the order information necessary for email notifications to the restaurant owner. Payment screenshots are not included in FormSubmit email notifications and are viewed inside the protected admin dashboard.

Pizza Dot manually reviews proof of payment. Do not submit passwords, government ID numbers or bank-card details in order notes or screenshots. Only include the relevant transaction details.

To request correction or removal of stored personal data, contact the restaurant using the contact links on the homepage. Data retention and removal are managed by the restaurant administrator; this website does not automatically delete submitted orders or screenshots.

This page describes the supplied website setup. The business owner must review and update the policy, retention periods and contact details before launching publicly.
